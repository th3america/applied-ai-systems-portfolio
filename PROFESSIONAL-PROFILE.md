# Jason Moore — professional AI positioning

**Applied AI Systems Architect | Agent Orchestration | Model Behavior Evaluation | AI Safety and Reliability**

## Professional summary

Independent applied-AI systems architect and behavioral evaluator specializing
in multi-agent orchestration, model reliability, AI safety, evidence-grounded
verification, and human–AI workflow design. Architects and directs the
implementation of governed AI systems that combine language models,
deterministic software, retrieval, operator controls, and auditable evidence.
Experienced in identifying failure modes such as hallucination, specification
gaming, reward hacking, sycophancy, authority drift, unsupported certainty, and
identity or attribution errors. Converts advanced AI concepts into testable
architectures, evaluation protocols, operating procedures, and working
prototypes.

## Professional value proposition

Jason's strongest contribution is not routine annotation. It is seeing the AI
system as a whole—model behavior, tools, memory, identity, permissions,
verification, operator control, failure recovery, and resource cost—and turning
that system-level understanding into explicit designs and tests.

He is particularly effective when a team needs someone who can:

- expose the difference between a convincing AI claim and verified behavior;
- find cross-layer failure modes that ordinary software QA or prompt review misses;
- design boundaries for autonomous or tool-using agents;
- coordinate multiple models and deterministic components without collapsing identity or authority;
- translate ambiguous behavioral goals into testable controls, rubrics, workflows, and acceptance criteria;
- preserve provenance, uncertainty, and human decision rights in AI-assisted operations.
