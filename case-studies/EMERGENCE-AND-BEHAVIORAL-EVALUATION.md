# Case study: emergence and behavioral evaluation

## Problem

Terms such as emergence, creativity, self-governance, or self-recognition are
often asserted from impressive outputs without separating mechanisms, observable
behavior, counterexamples, and reproducibility.

## Method

Jason decomposes high-level claims into functional behaviors, test conditions,
failure modes, acceptance evidence, and limits. Candidate behaviors remain
distinct from accepted capabilities. Contradiction, uncertainty, alternate
routes, and unresolved states stay visible rather than being compressed into a
forced success/failure label.

Applied work includes tests for self-directed task expansion, justified stopping,
creation/ownership recognition, long-horizon constraints, reward hacking,
specification gaming, sycophancy, semantic drift, and benchmark self-critique.

## Professional relevance

This maps to frontier-model evaluation, AI safety research operations,
behavioral red teaming, benchmark design, evaluation science, and complex-systems
analysis.
